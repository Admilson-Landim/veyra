Veyra is a music player for the audio files stored on your phone. No ads, no account, no internet connection: your music, playlists and settings never leave the device.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Finds the music on your device automatically and keeps up when files are added or removed.
• Browse by tracks, folders or artists, and sort by title, artist, album, date added or length.
• Search titles, artists, albums and folders as you type, ignoring accents and case.
• Tidy names: files without tags, such as "Artist_-_Song_(Official_Video).mp3", are shown as "Song" by "Artist". Tags with garbled characters ("JoÃ£o") are shown correctly ("João"). Your files are never changed.
• Hide folders you don't want, such as voice recordings, and skip very short tracks.
• Covers from the files (and, on Android 12 and older, from images in the same folder).

PLAYBACK
• Plays in the background, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
• Picks up the last queue where you left off.
• Shuffle, repeat one or all, and swipe the cover to change track.
• Set where each track starts and ends, or skip the first or last seconds of every track.
• Skip silence.
• Sleep timer: 15 to 90 minutes, a custom time or the end of the current track, with a gentle fade-out.

SOUND
• Equalizer with your device's bands and presets, plus bass boost.
• Balanced volume between tracks, using ReplayGain tags when present.
(Audio effects depend on the device; some manufacturers ignore or replace them.)

MORE
• Playlists: create, rename, reorder and play.
• Set a song, or just part of it, as your ringtone, notification or alarm sound.
• Remove a track from the device: moved to the system trash on Android 11 and later.
• Dark and light themes, in English and Portuguese.

Veyra plays the audio files already on your device. It does not stream, download or sell music.
